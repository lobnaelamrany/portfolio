01 About

About Me

I am a Full-Stack Development graduate from Fès, Morocco. I completed my training in Digital Development – Web Full Stack at OFPPT, where I learned how to build web applications from the database to the user interface.

During my training, I worked on several projects using PHP, Laravel, MySQL, JavaScript, and React. I enjoy the process of turning an idea into a working product: designing the database, writing backend logic, and building a clear, usable interface.

I am now looking for an opportunity to begin my Ausbildung as a Fachinformatikerin für Anwendungsentwicklung in Germany. I want to learn from experienced developers and grow in a professional environment.

02 Why Germany?

Why Germany?

The German dual training system combines theory and practice in a way that makes sense to me. You learn at school, and you apply what you learn in a real company.

I want to work on real projects, learn from experienced developers, and understand how software is built in a structured company. This is the kind of learning I am looking for.

Germany has a strong engineering culture and high standards for quality. I want to grow in this environment, both as a developer and as a person.

Cosmetics E-Commerce Platform

  • Laravel 12
  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • Bootstrap
Completed ↓

An online cosmetics shop built with Laravel. Customers can browse products, place orders, and track them. Admins can manage products, orders, stock, and suppliers.

Problem

Small cosmetics shops in Morocco often track stock and orders manually. This causes stock errors and lost sales.

Solution

The shop brings products, orders, stock, and suppliers into one place, with an admin panel for daily management.

Roles

Customer, Employee, Admin

My Contribution

  • MySQL 8

Fixora

Service Booking Platform

  • Laravel 12
  • Tailwind
  • Alpine.js
  • MySQL
In Development ↓

A platform that connects customers with local service professionals. Customers send requests, professionals accept them, and both can chat inside the platform.

Problem

Finding a reliable repair professional is hard. People rely on word of mouth and have no way to compare or track requests.

Solution

Roles

My Contribution

  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Alpine.js
  • Vite

What I Learned

Hotel Management System

  • PHP
  • MySQL
  • Bootstrap
Main Modules ↓

A hotel management app built during my internship at Medina Click as my final-year project. It handles room bookings, restaurant orders, and guest services. This was a team project.
